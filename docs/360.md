## 1. Summary

**Client.** Sports Nutrition Hub (SNH) is a Sri Lankan supplement retailer. It sells online at www.sportsnutritionhub.lk on Shopify (LKR, standard Shopify plan, not Plus) and in stores at Dehiwala, Nugegoda and Wattala, plus a warehouse location *[live 22 Nov: Shopify shop + locations]*.

**Goal.** Replace a heavily patched Impact theme with a fast, clear, mobile-first store, then add what drives repeat orders:
- bundles
- the SNH Elite loyalty programme
- email automation
- click & collect
- an internal dashboard

- **Engine:** `assets/snh-search.js`, loaded only when search is opened. *[SEARCH-RESTORE.md]*
- **Matching:**
  - typo tolerance, brand shorthand and a relevance floor
  - "tablets" = "capsules"
  - two-word brands match as a whole
  - "Closest matches" instead of a blank page
- **Ordering:**
  - in-stock products before sold-out ones
  - a bundle named after the search is lifted to position 2
- **Catalogue:** cached for 30 minutes in the browser.
- **Prefix matching (17 Nov):** the word being typed counts from 2 letters; a single trailing letter only nudges the order *[SEARCH-RESTORE.md, 51d5522]*.
- **Restock WhatsApp:** sold-out product pages show "Update me when restocked", which opens WhatsApp to the restock number. Setting: SNH contact and reviews → Restock WhatsApp number *[SEARCH-RESTORE.md]*.
- **Speed:** no load cost; home Lighthouse stayed within noise *[SEARCH-RESTORE.md]*.
