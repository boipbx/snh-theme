# SNH store: before and after

**Before** is the previous theme, "New store Menu launch" (#154272432382), viewed through its read-only preview link. The library has no theme named "Updated copy of Impact", so this is the oldest previous theme still there. **After** is the live v2 theme. All screenshots were taken on 20 Nov 2026 at phone width (375px) and desktop width (1440px). Nothing on the live store was changed. Any email address or phone number on screen is masked.

## Screens without a "before" (skipped, not mocked up)

- **16-banner-snh-elite (mobile, desktop):** the old theme has no SNH Elite section on its homepage, so there is nothing to pair.
- **Account dashboard and orders page (all widths):** no signed-in customer session was available. Login is behind a captcha, and creating an account just to take screenshots would change the live store.
- **Collection banners:** the Bundle & Save, SNH Elite and RYSE banners were captured on the homepage. The collection-page header appears in 08.
- **Welcome or first-order notification:** no separate "first order" notice appears on screen in either theme. The welcome popup is in 04.

## Customer-facing features added in v2 that the screenshots don't show

- **Welcome flow:** new subscribers get a Klaviyo welcome email series, alongside abandoned-cart, abandoned-checkout, post-purchase and how-to-use emails.
- **SNH Elite rewards:** a free loyalty club with 500 welcome points, Bronze/Silver/Gold tiers and 3–10% back in points on every order.
- **My rewards in the account:** members see their points balance, tier and progress, with tips on how to spend points.
- **Points redemption:** members can turn points into a discount code. It is built, and the public button stays paused until switched on after testing.
- **28 ready-made bundles:** stacks like endurance, daily health and strength sell at one bundle price, with the saving shown on every card.
- **"Save with a bundle" on product pages:** if a product is part of a bundle, the bundle and the saving appear right on its page.
- **Click & collect:** free pickup at Nugegoda, with the order held for 48 hours, offered in the basket.
- **Delivery date estimate:** the basket shows same-day delivery in Colombo and a dated estimate for the rest of Sri Lanka.
- **Koko pay-in-3:** "or 3 × Rs …" with the Koko logo on product cards, product pages and the basket, so shoppers see the instalment price up front.
- **Free-delivery progress bar:** the basket shows how much more to spend for free delivery, then a congratulations message.
- **Smart basket suggestions:** "Goes with your order" picks small add-ons that fit the basket.
- **Product finder quiz:** "Not sure what to buy? Get your plan in 60 seconds."
- **Compare similar products:** shoppers pick other brands in the same category and see the differences side by side.
- **Informed Sport badges:** batch-tested products are marked on cards and get their own homepage section.
- **Brand logos:** product pages show the brand's logo, which links to that brand's collection.
- **Full-screen zoomable gallery:** product photos open full screen with pinch zoom, and detail images can be opened the same way.
- **Sunday SiS online deal:** on Sundays a small gift chip opens a spin wheel that gives 15% off SiS, with an "Apply to my cart" button.
- **Customer photo and video reviews:** reviews show on product pages, with a "Write a review" button.
- **Store and contact section:** three stores, with addresses, phone and WhatsApp, appear on the homepage and in the footer.
- **"Grow with SNH":** a wholesale and partnership section for gyms and stores.
- **TikTok and Instagram sections:** recent videos and posts appear on the homepage.
- **Branded Shopify emails:** account welcome, order confirmation, shipping and password reset emails are prepared in SNH branding and added to Shopify by hand.
