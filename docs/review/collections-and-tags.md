# Collections & tags blueprint

**Sports Nutrition Hub · new website launch** · prepared 7 November 2026

This is the full map of how products are organised on the new website: every menu link, the collection behind it and how many products it holds today. It then proposes what to fix, merge and create, and how to tidy the product tags. **Khizer's part is the approval list on the last page.** Everything here was read from the live store and the new website's menus today.

**At a glance:** 252 products · 100 collections (5 of them empty) · 176 different product tags (61 used on only one product).

## 2. Collections that overlap

Several collections hold almost the same products. Shoppers see the same page twice under different names, and you have more to maintain.

| Collections | Products shared | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Stimulant (15) and With Caffeine (12) | 12 | Keep **With Caffeine**, retire Stimulant (not in any menu) |
| Non-Stim (10) and Without Caffeine (11) | 9 | Keep **Without Caffeine**, move the 1 extra product, retire Non-Stim |
| Beauty & Skin Care (9) and Beauty, Hair & Skin (12) | 6 | Keep **Beauty, Hair & Skin**, add the 3 extras, retire Beauty & Skin Care |
| Hair Supplement (6) and Skin & Nails Supplement (6) | 6 (identical) | Fold both into **Beauty, Hair & Skin** |
| BCAA & EAA (3) and Aminos & BCAA (6) | 3 | Keep **Aminos & BCAA** and point both menu links to it |

| Fat Burners (12), Fat Loss (24), Weight Management (29) | all 12 / all 24 | Keep all three: each is a step wider than the last |

## 4. Product tags

There are **176 different tags** on 252 products. Tags drive the automatic collections, so messy tags mean products quietly go missing from collections.

**How the tags break down today**

| Kind of tag | Examples | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Category (build the collections) | Whey Proteins (34), Pre-Workout (27), Creatine (24), Sleep Health (19), Informed Sports (54) | The core. Keep. |
| Goal / range | Train & Perform (137), Muscle & Strength (87), Health & Wellness (93), Energy & Endurance (50) | Keep. These power the big menu sections. |
| Feature | With Caffeine, No Caffeine, STIMULANT, NON-STIM, FLAVORED, UNFLAVORED | Keep one pair per idea (see section 2) |
| Brand | REVIVE (12), SiS (4), Applied Nutrition (3), RAW Nutrition (3), RYSE, ABE | Not needed: brands already come from each product's brand (vendor) field. Remove. |
| Size / format | 90 Capsules, 30 Servings, TABLET FORM, 132g, Powder, sample, trial | Not used anywhere on the site. Remove. |
| Lower-case duplicates | daily-wellness, whey-protein, gut-health, energy-gel … (38 tags) | Older copies of existing tags. Merge into the main tag. |

**Duplicates to merge (same idea, different spelling)**

| Keep | Merge in |

**Tags only partly applied.** Some collections hold far more products than carry the matching tag: Minerals (27 products, 13 tagged), Letter Vitamins (21, 10 tagged), Magnesium (20, 9 tagged), Ashwagandha (13, 5 tagged), Herbs & Natural (21, 11 tagged). Once tags are tidied, these collections can fill themselves automatically instead of by hand.

**Tags the new website reads:** *Informed Sports* (shows the Informed Sport badge; already on 54 products, works today) and *bestseller* (shows a Bestseller badge; on no products yet).
