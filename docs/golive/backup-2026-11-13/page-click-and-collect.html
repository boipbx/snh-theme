Order online, collect at SNH Nugegoda. Free, and held for 48 hours.

How it works

  1. Order online. Add your products to the basket and go to checkout.
  2. Choose Pickup. At checkout, pick Pickup instead of delivery and choose your outlet. Outlets are only shown when they have everything in your basket.
  3. We message you. We'll let you know as soon as your order is ready, usually within 24 hours.
  4. Collect. Come to the store and show your order number.

The 48-hour rule

We hold your order for 48 hours after we message you that it's ready. Unpaid orders that are not collected within 48 hours are cancelled automatically, so the stock is free for other customers. Orders you have already paid for are never cancelled automatically; we'll get in touch with you instead.

Need more time? WhatsApp us on +94 77 977 3359 and we'll keep it for you.

Payment

Pay online by card or Koko when you order, or pay at the store when you collect if that option is shown at checkout.

FAQ

Can someone else collect my order?
Yes. Send them with your order number and let us know their name on WhatsApp.

Can I change the outlet?
Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible and we'll help if the stock is available there.

Can I get more time to collect?
Yes. WhatsApp us on +94 77 977 3359 before the 48 hours are up and we'll extend the hold.

Is click & collect free?
Yes, there is no charge for picking up your order.
