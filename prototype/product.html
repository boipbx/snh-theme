        Description

        A premium daily whey protein built around a multi-source dairy blend — designed for serious lifters, athletes, active adults, and anyone whose daily protein intake is the foundation of their muscle building, recovery, or body composition goals. Particularly suited to those wanting a more credible alternative to budget single-source whey concentrate, lifters running heavy training programmes, anyone whose current protein sits at a poor protein-to-calorie ratio, and people seeking the highest level of third-party banned-substance testing available.

        What separates Applied Nutrition Critical Whey from the typical whey product on the market comes down to protein blend quality and macro efficiency. Each serving delivers 24g of high-quality protein from a scientifically-formulated four-source blend: Whey Protein Concentrate, Whey Protein Isolate, Hydrolysed Whey, and Milk Protein Concentrate — all sourced from grass-fed dairy. This blend matters because each protein source absorbs at a different rate: hydrolysed whey is the fastest, isolate and concentrate are moderate, and the milk protein concentrate (which includes casein) is slower-digesting. The result is a sustained amino acid release curve rather than a single rapid spike that dissipates within an hour — meaningful for post-workout recovery and overnight protein support. The macro profile is equally important: 24g of protein at just 120 calories per serving delivers an exceptional protein-to-calorie ratio that fits both bulking and cutting phases. The product is also Informed Protein tested for protein accuracy (the actual protein content matches the label) — a meaningful detail in a market where some commodity products underdeliver on stated protein. Available in flavours tested as Informed-Sport certified (Chocolate Milkshake, Strawberry Milkshake, Vanilla Ice Cream), giving competing athletes confidence in batch-by-batch banned-substance testing.

        Applied Nutrition manufactures Critical Whey in the UK in facilities certified to GMP standards, with rigorous quality control across the range. Importantly, every batch is Informed Sport tested — meaning batches are independently tested for WADA-banned substances at an ISO 17025 accredited lab. For competing tested-pool athletes in Sri Lanka, that's the highest level of third-party verification available.

        How to use

        Add one scoop (around 30g) to 250–300ml of cold water or milk in a shaker, seal, and shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds. Use cold water for a leaner profile and faster mixing, or milk for additional calories and creaminess. Take any time of day to support your daily protein target, particularly within 30–60 minutes post-workout, between meals, or as a high-protein snack on the go.
